## Expected QR Code Features

The Lark QR code should:

- Display the Lark group join QR code
- Be in PNG format
- Have minimum dimensions of 300x300 pixels
- Be clearly readable when printed or viewed on mobile

## How to Join

1. Open the Lark app on your device
2. Scan the QR code above
3. Complete the verification if prompted
4. Introduce yourself in the group

## Group Purpose

This Lark group serves as the primary English-language community channel for:

- KALLAX project announcements
- Feature discussions and decisions
- Developer support and Q&A
- Showcases and use case sharing
